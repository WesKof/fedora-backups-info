Privacy notice

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Purpose and access

Fedora Backups is operated by its owner for personal backups. It uses the Google Drive API through rclone to create, list, retrieve, and manage backup files accessible through the drive.file permission.

Data handling

Backup contents and filenames are encrypted locally by restic before upload. Google receives the encrypted repository objects and normal service metadata such as object sizes and transfer times. Authentication credentials are stored locally and used to communicate with Google.

This utility does not send backup data to the restic or rclone developers and does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Control and retention

The owner controls the backup schedule and retention settings. Access can be revoked in the Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing backup objects; the owner can manage or delete them in Google Drive.

This website

These informational pages contain no signup form, analytics scripts, or credentials. The hosting provider may process standard connection and request logs.
